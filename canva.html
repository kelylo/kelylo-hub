    Canva 80/20 Roadmap

    Total time: 2-3 weeks at 3 hours/day.

    Core philosophy: Canva is for fast, professional engineering visuals, not full graphic design.

    Week 1: Foundation & Branding

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 1 Interface & Templates Templates save 90% of design time. Explore templates for Presentation, Resume, Social Media.
    Day 2 Brand Kit Consistent colors, fonts, logo. Create a brand kit with 3 colors and 2 fonts.
    Day 3 Magic Studio AI Tools AI generates editable designs fast. Use Magic Design for an engineering proposal template.
    Day 4 Background Remover Clean profile and site photos. Remove background from a project photo.
    Day 5 Frames & Grids Perfect collages without manual alignment. Create a 6-photo grid with captions.
    Day 6 Elements: Shapes, Lines, Icons Build diagrams and flowcharts. Create a 5-step construction process flow.
    Day 7 Project: Personal Brand Kit Your visual identity. Logo, LinkedIn banner, resume header, email signature.

    Week 2: Visual Communication for Engineers

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 8 Charts & Graphs Presentation-ready visuals from data. Import data and build a bar chart.
    Day 9 Magic Write (AI Copy) Instant project descriptions. Generate a 3-sentence portfolio description.
    Day 10 Magic Resize One design, many sizes. Resize a project update to all social sizes.
    Day 11 Pages & Folders Organize multi-page documents. Create a 5-page portfolio layout.
    Day 12 Align, Distribute & Group Professional spacing. Align 10 elements and group them.
    Day 13 Transparency & Layering Depth and readable overlays. Layer text with a semi-transparent shape.
    Day 14 Project: Engineering Portfolio (Part 1) 5-page visual portfolio. Title, About, 3 projects with visuals.

    Week 3: Advanced Communication & Video

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 15 Video Editor Create walkthroughs fast. Trim 3 clips and add transitions.
    Day 16 Beat Sync & Captions Sync audio and add accessibility. Add music, Beat Sync, auto-captions.
    Day 17 Presenter Notes Present cleanly with notes. Create a 5-slide pitch with notes.
    Day 18 Record & Present Video pitch or client update. Record your 5-slide deck, export MP4.
    Day 19 Canva Sheets & Live Data Visual dashboards linked to data. Create a cost tracking sheet with charts.
    Day 20 QR Codes & Integrations Interactive portfolio assets. Create a QR code to your LinkedIn.
    Day 21 Final Project Portfolio + video package. 10-page portfolio, 2-min intro video, exports.

    Daily Practice Routine

    1. Open the software.
    2. Review yesterday's skills (5 min).
    3. Watch a tutorial on today's feature (10 min).
    4. Replicate exactly what you saw (10 min).
