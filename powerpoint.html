    Microsoft PowerPoint 80/20 Roadmap

    Total time: 2-3 weeks at 3 hours/day.

    Core philosophy: 20% of features deliver 80% of presentation impact.

    Week 1: Structure & Efficiency

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 1 Slide Master Change one master slide, update all slides. Create a custom layout with logo, colors, fonts.
    Day 2 Layouts & Placeholders Content flows in consistent containers. Create a layout with a picture placeholder.
    Day 3 Themes & Variants One click changes the whole deck. Apply themes and customize colors.
    Day 4 Outlining in Word/Navigation Structure before design. Import a Word outline into PowerPoint.
    Day 5 Section Breaks Organize large decks. Create sections and reorder them.
    Day 6 Selection Pane Manage layers and hidden objects. Use Selection Pane on a slide with 10 objects.
    Day 7 Project: Master Deck Create a reusable template. Build a master deck with 5 custom layouts.

    Week 2: Visual Communication

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 8 SmartArt Convert bullets into diagrams. Convert a 5-item list into SmartArt.
    Day 9 Charts & Tables Linked data that updates. Link an Excel chart and format a table.
    Day 10 Images: Format & Crop Polished visuals fast. Remove background, crop to shape, add shadow.
    Day 11 Icons & 3D Models Modern, scalable graphics. Insert icons and a 3D model.
    Day 12 Align & Distribute Professional spacing. Align and distribute shapes, then group them.
    Day 13 Format Painter & Eyedropper Copy styles instantly. Use Format Painter on 10 objects.
    Day 14 Project: Technical Presentation 10-slide engineering presentation. Use SmartArt, charts, images, alignment.

    Week 3: Delivery & Polish

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 15 Animations (Basic) Reveal complex info cleanly. Add fade animations to bullet points.
    Day 16 Transitions (Subtle) Professional flow with Morph/Fade. Apply Morph between two slides.
    Day 17 Presenter View Notes, timer, next slide. Practice with notes using Presenter View.
    Day 18 Rehearse with Coach Feedback on pacing and delivery. Run Rehearse with Coach and adjust.
    Day 19 Recording & Narration Export as video with narration. Record narration and export MP4.
    Day 20 Export Options PDF handouts and packaging. Export handouts and package presentation.
    Day 21 Final Project Deliver a 15-minute presentation. Use masters, visuals, notes, and export.

    Daily Practice Routine

    1. Open the software.
    2. Review yesterday's skills (5 min).
    3. Watch a tutorial on today's feature (10 min).
    4. Replicate exactly what you saw (10 min).
    5. Apply it to your own project (5 min).

    Next 2 hours: Work through hands-on tasks. Break the feature, fix it, understand it.

    Last 30 minutes: Self-test from memory. Write down one insight. Preview tomorrow.
