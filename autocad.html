    AutoCAD 80/20 Roadmap

    Total time: 6-8 weeks at 3 hours/day.

    Core philosophy: AutoCAD is about workflow speed and drawing intelligence. Master the few commands that do most of the work.

    Phase 1: Foundation & Interface (Weeks 1-2)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 1Interface & WorkspaceKnow where everything lives.Explore Ribbon, Command Line, Model/Layout tabs.
    Day 2Zoom (Z) & PanNavigate without losing your place.Practice Zoom Extents, Window, and Pan.
    Day 3Units & LimitsPrevent scale disasters.Set units and limits for a new drawing.
    Day 4Line (L) & CoordinatesFoundation of drafting.Draw a 10x10 square using coordinate methods.
    Day 5Circle/Arc/RectangleBasic geometry for elements.Draw columns, beams, and curved walls.
    Day 6Polyline (PL)Single connected object.Draw a floor outline as one polyline.
    Day 7Project: Simple Floor PlanCombine basics into a layout.Draw walls, doors, and windows. Save as DWG.

    Phase 2: Precision & Editing (Weeks 3-4)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 8Object Snaps (OSNAP)Professional precision.Set running snaps and practice.
    Day 9Ortho (F8) & Polar (F10)Controlled angles.Draw with Ortho, then Polar at 30 degrees.
    Day 10Selection MethodsWindow vs Crossing.Practice selection differences.
    Day 11Trim (TR) & Extend (EX)Clean intersections.Trim and extend intersecting lines.
    Day 12Offset (O)Parallel lines fast.Offset centerlines to create walls.
    Day 13Move/Copy/RotateAdjust without redrawing.Copy a column grid, move and rotate elements.
    Day 14Mirror & ArraySymmetry and repetition.Mirror half a plan and array columns.

    Phase 3: Organization & Intelligence (Weeks 5-6)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 15Layers (LA)Keep drawings readable.Create layers and assign objects.
    Day 16Layer StatesControl visibility/printing.Freeze and lock layers.
    Day 17Properties & MatchpropEdit and copy properties.Change lineweights and match properties.
    Day 18Blocks & InsertReusable symbols.Create and insert a door block.
    Day 19WBlockReusable files across projects.Export a block and reinsert it.
    Day 20Hatch (H)Material indication.Hatch concrete and steel patterns.
    Day 21Project: Structural GridGrid with blocks and hatch.Create grid, insert columns, hatch.

    Phase 4: Annotation & Communication (Weeks 7-8)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 22Text & MTextLabels and notes.Add room labels and notes.
    Day 23Text StylesConsistency.Create Title and Notes styles.
    Day 24DimensioningConvey size.Dimension the floor plan.
    Day 25Dimension StylesControl arrows and text.Create two dimension styles.
    Day 26Leader & MultileaderConnect notes.Add leader to column note.
    Day 27Annotative ScalingText resizes by viewport.Set annotative scales 1:50 and 1:100.
    Day 28Project: Annotated DrawingComplete floor plan.Add dimensions, notes, title block.

    Phase 5: Paper Space & Output (Weeks 9-10)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 29Model Space vs Paper SpaceProfessional plotting.Explain the difference in your notes.
    Day 30Layouts & ViewportsMultiple views on one sheet.Create layout and viewport.
    Day 31Viewport ScalingCorrect print scales.Set 1:50 scale and lock viewport.
    Day 32Viewport LayersControl visibility per view.Freeze layers per viewport.
    Day 33Title BlocksProfessional presentation.Create title block with attributes.
    Day 34Page SetupPlot settings.Create page setup for A1/ARCH D.
    Day 35Plot to PDFDeliverable output.Plot and check lineweights.
    Day 36Project: Drawing SetMulti-view deliverable.Create plan, detail, section views.

    Phase 6: Advanced Efficiency (Weeks 11-12)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 37Fillet & ChamferQuick corner control.Fillet radius 0 and radius 2.
    Day 38StretchResize without redrawing.Stretch a room longer.
    Day 39Break & JoinSplit or combine objects.Break a line and join it.
    Day 40ExplodeEdit block components.Explode a block and edit lines.
    Day 41PurgeClean file size.Purge unused items.
    Day 42XREFCollaborate with references.XREF an architectural base file.
    Day 43Design CenterReuse content across files.Import blocks from another drawing.
    Day 44Tool PalettesOne-click inserts.Create palette with column block.
    Day 45Quick SelectBulk edits by property.Select all A-ANNO text and recolor.
    Day 46FieldDynamic text updates.Add date and sheet number fields.
    Day 47DWG Convert & eTransmitPackage for sharing.Create an eTransmit package.
    Day 48Final ProjectComplete structural set.3-sheet set with plots to PDF.
