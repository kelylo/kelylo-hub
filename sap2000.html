    SAP2000 80/20 Roadmap

    Total time: 6-8 weeks at 3 hours/day.

    Core philosophy: Define -> Draw -> Assign -> Analyze -> Design.

    Phase 1: Foundation & Interface (Weeks 1-2)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 1Interface OrientationUnderstand main panes.Identify Model Explorer, Properties, Displays.
    Day 2Units & PreferencesUnits must be correct upfront.Set kN, m, C. Choose design code.
    Day 3Grid SystemDefine structural geometry.Create 4x4 grid and story levels.
    Day 4Material DefinitionsRealistic properties.Define concrete, steel, rebar.
    Day 5Frame SectionsProper member properties.Add W18x35, W12x65, 20x20 section.
    Day 6Drawing ObjectsBuild basic models.Draw a simple portal frame.
    Day 7Project: Simple 2D FrameComplete first model.2-bay, 2-story frame with pinned base.

    Phase 2: Loading & Combinations (Weeks 3-4)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 8Load PatternsSeparate dead/live/wind.Create DEAD, LIVE, WINDX, WINDY.
    Day 9Loads to FramesDistributed and point loads.Apply DEAD and LIVE to beams.
    Day 10Area LoadsUniform loads to frames.Create slab and apply 5 kN/m2.
    Day 11Load CombinationsManual understanding.Create 1.4D, 1.2D+1.6L, 1.2D+1.0L+1.0W.
    Day 12Auto Load CombinationsCode-based combos.Enable auto combos for ASCE 7.
    Day 13Wind Load GenerationAuto wind loading.Define exposure and generate wind loads.
    Day 14Project: Fully Loaded FrameComplete load setup.Apply all loads and verify combos.

    Phase 3: Analysis & Results (Weeks 5-6)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 15Running AnalysisCheck options and warnings.Run linear static, review log.
    Day 16Deformed ShapeBehavior verification.View deformed shape with scale factor.
    Day 17Force DiagramsMoments and shear.Display moment and shear diagrams.
    Day 18Section CutsBase shear and core forces.Create section cut and review forces.
    Day 19Joint ReactionsFoundation forces.Display joint reactions at supports.
    Day 20Display TablesExport to Excel.Open tables and copy member forces.
    Day 21Project: Results SummaryDocument results.Create PDF with deflections, moments, tables.

    Phase 4: Design & Code-Checking (Weeks 7-8)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 22Steel Design PreferencesCorrect design method.Select AISC 360-16 LRFD.
    Day 23Steel Design GroupsEfficient checks.Group beams and columns.
    Day 24Run Steel DesignCheck utilization.Review PMM interaction ratios.
    Day 25Interactive DesignOptimized sections.Auto-select lightest passing sections.
    Day 26Concrete BeamsFlexure and shear design.Review required rebar and stirrups.
    Day 27Concrete ColumnsInteraction checks.Review interaction ratio and rebar.
    Day 28Project: Designed StructureComplete design cycle.All members pass 0.3-0.9 utilization.

    Phase 5: Advanced & Integration (Weeks 9-10)

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 29Modal AnalysisDynamic behavior.Review first 3 modes and periods.
    Day 30Response SpectrumSeismic loading.Define spectrum and run analysis.
    Day 31Time History AnalysisSpecific earthquake records.Import accelerogram and analyze.
    Day 32P-Delta AnalysisSecond-order effects.Compare with first-order analysis.
    Day 33Pushover AnalysisNonlinear capacity.Define hinges and review capacity curve.
    Day 34Import from DXFCAD to model.Import DXF as SAP2000 model.
    Day 35Export to ExcelReporting dashboards.Export forces to Excel and summarize.
    Day 36Parametric Modeling (API)Automation with scripts.Create a simple model via API.
    Day 37Project: 3D Steel BuildingFull steel workflow.Model, load, analyze, design.
    Day 38Project: Concrete Shear WallWall design and drift.Model and design walls and frames.
    Day 39Bridge ModelingMoving loads.Create bridge model and influence lines.
    Day 40Final CapstoneMixed structure.Full loads, seismic, design, report.

    The Truth About Structural Software

    Software amplifies engineering judgment. Always check if results make sense.

    • Does this result make sense?
    • Is the moment diagram shape correct?
    • Why is this member failing?
    • What happens if I change this support?
