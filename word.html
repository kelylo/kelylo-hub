    Microsoft Word 80/20 Roadmap

    Total time: 2-4 weeks at 3 hours/day.

    Core philosophy: Most people use only 20% of Word. The other 80% separates beginners from professionals.

    Week 1: The Foundation That 90% of Users Never Learn

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 1 Styles Format entire documents fast and enable TOC. Create Heading 1/2 and Normal styles. Apply to a sample document.
    Day 2 Navigation Pane Reorder sections by dragging headings. Turn on Navigation Pane. Reorganize a 10-page document.
    Day 3 Page Setup & Breaks Different headers/footers and orientations per section. Insert Next Page breaks. Make one section landscape.
    Day 4 Headers & Footers Different first/odd/even pages, restart numbering. Create headers per section and restart page numbers.
    Day 5 Table of Contents Automatic TOC updates in one click. Insert automatic TOC and update it after edits.
    Day 6 Lists & Multilevel Lists Professional numbering (1, 1.1, 1.1.1). Create a multilevel list linked to Heading styles.
    Day 7 Project: Report Template Combine everything into reusable templates. Build a report template and save as .dotx.

    Week 2: Professional Document Features

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 8 Tables Engineering data formatting and repeat headers. Merge headers, sort data, repeat header row.
    Day 9 Pictures & SmartArt Process diagrams and polished visuals. Insert a picture, apply style, create a flowchart.
    Day 10 Captions & Cross-References Auto-number figures and reference them. Add captions and cross-references, then reorder.
    Day 11 Footnotes & Endnotes Academic and technical writing essentials. Insert footnotes and endnotes, customize format.
    Day 12 Citations & Bibliography Auto-generate sources and references. Add sources, insert citations, build bibliography.
    Day 13 Find, Replace & Go To Advanced formatting search/replace. Replace all instances of a word/style.
    Day 14 Project: Engineering Report Full professional report workflow. Write a 5-page report with TOC, tables, figures.

    Week 3: Efficiency & Automation

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 15 Tabs & Indents Professional layout control. Set tabs and build a dot-leader TOC manually.
    Day 16 Columns Newsletter-style layouts. Create a two-column section with column breaks.
    Day 17 Mail Merge Mass-generate letters, emails, labels. Merge a letter with a list of 10 names.
    Day 18 Track Changes & Comments Collaborative editing workflow. Practice accept/reject changes and comments.
    Day 19 Comparing Documents See changes between versions. Compare two versions and review differences.
    Day 20 Forms & Content Controls Create fillable inspection forms. Build a form with checkboxes and date fields.
    Day 21 Project: Collaborative Document Simulate team workflow. Review comments and finalize a shared document.

    Week 4: Mastery & Review

    Day 20% Feature Why It Matters Hands-On Task
    Day 22 Keyboard Shortcuts Speed separates amateurs from pros. Use core shortcuts for a full day.
    Day 23 Quick Parts Reusable content blocks. Create a disclaimer Quick Part.
    Day 24 Macros (Basic) Automate repetitive formatting. Record a macro for table formatting.
    Day 25 Document Properties & Fields Auto-update titles, dates, authors. Insert document fields and update them.
    Day 26 Review & Protect Restrict editing and secure documents. Protect a document and restrict formatting.
    Day 27 Accessibility & Export Accessible PDFs with bookmarks. Run Accessibility Checker and export to PDF.
    Day 28 Final Project End-to-end mastery proof. Write a 20-30 page document using all features.

    Daily Practice Routine

    1. Open the software.
    2. Review yesterday's skills (5 min).
    3. Watch a tutorial on today's feature (10 min).
    4. Replicate exactly what you saw (10 min).
    5. Apply it to your own project (5 min).

    Next 2 hours: Work through hands-on tasks. Break the feature, fix it, understand it.

    Last 30 minutes: Self-test from memory. Write down one insight. Preview tomorrow.
